Digital Product Factory

Original digital products, built on careful research.

Digital Product Factory is a small independent project that designs and sells original digital products, such as printable and downloadable resources, through online marketplaces.

Our internal research tool

We are developing Digital Product Factory Research, an internal tool used only by our own team. It helps us understand market demand and competition so we can decide which original products are worth creating.

  • It works with publicly available marketplace information, such as listings, prices and ratings.
  • It is not offered to other users and is not sold as a service.
  • It does not copy or resell other sellers' products or content.

Platform policies

We access marketplace data only through official, authorized APIs, and we follow each platform's terms of use, rate limits and data policies.
